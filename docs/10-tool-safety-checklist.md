# The Tool-Safety Checklist

Print this chapter and tape it near your desk. It's the whole guide compressed into checkboxes — run it before you use a new tool, before you generate, before you share, and before you print.

## Before using a new tool

- [ ] I checked the tool's terms of service for **commercial use**, **ownership**, and **watermarks** (Chapter 6).
- [ ] I checked the **privacy/training settings** — is my prompt and any uploaded image kept or used for training? Can I turn that off? (Chapters 1 and 8)
- [ ] I know what the **free tier actually costs** (limits, watermarks, rights limits) before entering a payment card.
- [ ] I only enter payment details on a site I trust — correct address (https, no lookalike domains), not a copycat ad link.
- [ ] For family photos and faces: I prefer tools that **don't store uploads**, or a local/offline tool. (Chapter 8)

## Before generating

- [ ] My prompt doesn't ask for a **real, identifiable person** without genuine permission — and never for anything in the zero-tolerance zone. (Chapter 7)
- [ ] I'm not uploading someone else's artwork/photo/logo without their OK or a license. (Chapter 6)
- [ ] I've set the **aspect ratio** and lighting/framing words deliberately (Chapter 2) — vague prompts are silent decisions.
- [ ] For text in the image: I've minimized it or planned to add it in a graphics app. (Chapter 5)

## Before editing or upscaling

- [ ] I'm changing **one thing per attempt** (one word, one slider, one mask). (Chapter 3)
- [ ] Faces get a **side-by-side check** after upscaling, especially real people. (Chapter 4)
- [ ] I looked at the full image at **100% zoom** for hands, text, mirrors, and crowd faces. (Chapter 5)
- [ ] The file has a **real name** and lives in a real folder — not `download(17).png`. (Chapter 4)

## Before sharing

- [ ] The image is **labeled as AI-generated** wherever it appears (caption, filename, or post text). (Chapter 7)
- [ ] Nothing could make a viewer believe a real person did/said/wore something they didn't. (Chapter 7)
- [ ] Family members in the image **consented** to this use, and kids' images got extra care. (Chapter 8)
- [ ] No one's watermark was cropped, covered, or removed — mine or anyone's. (Chapter 6)

## Before printing

- [ ] Pixel size covers **300 DPI** at my final print size (I did the math from Chapter 9).
- [ ] Design respects **margins/bleed** for this paper and printer.
- [ ] I ran the **test print on plain paper** first and checked at arm's length and 100% zoom.
- [ ] For selling anything: I confirmed **commercial rights** in my tool's terms and kept my little paper trail (tool, date, prompt). (Chapter 6)

## The 10-second version

> Terms read, uploads cleared, people consented, image labeled, test print done. If all five are true, ship it.
